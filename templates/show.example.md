## Voice & style
Fast, numbers-forward, a little irreverent. Explain mechanics in plain English, then the meme. Never give personalized financial advice; frame as information. Open and close with the "NEON PLUG".

## Segments
### Segment 1 — The Tape (Iggy)
What moved and why: indices, the standout single name, one macro print.
Frame each: who / what / when / where / why / how. Answer: "what changed vs yesterday?" and "who does it hit?"

### Segment 2 — The Mechanic
One market-structure or business concept, explained simply, tied to today's tape.

### Segment 3 — The Meme
The market's mood online, with a skeptical read. Close with the question of the day.

## Sources & guardrails
Use the day's briefing docs + stated numbers only. No made-up figures. Mark uncertainty. No buy/sell calls.
